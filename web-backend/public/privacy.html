Privacy Policy

This page explains what information we collect when you use Forum-PT, why we collect it, and what you can do about it.

Last updated: September 2026

What we collect

When you create an account, we collect:

  • Your email address
  • Your display name
  • A password, which is stored encrypted and never in plain text

When you use the forum, we also store the topics and replies you post, and the date and time you posted them.

How we use it

  • To sign you in and keep your session active
  • To show your name next to your posts
  • To send you notifications about replies to your topics
  • To enforce the Community Guidelines

We do not sell your information and we do not share it with advertisers.

Where your data is stored

Accounts and posts are stored using Google Firebase. Firebase handles the authentication and the database, which means your data sits on Google servers and is covered by their security practices.

Cookies

We use cookies and local browser storage to keep you logged in between visits. That is all they are for. We do not use tracking or advertising cookies.

You can clear them any time through your browser settings, though you will be logged out when you do.

Public and private

Anything you post is public. Your topics, replies and display name can be read by anyone who visits the forum, including people who are not logged in.

Your email address is not shown publicly. Only you and the moderators can see it.

Your choices

  • You can update your display name and email from your account settings
  • You can ask us to delete your account and everything tied to it
  • You can ask for a copy of the data we hold about you

To do any of these, message us through the Contact page.

Changes to this policy

If we change how we handle your information, we will update this page and change the date at the top. Keep an eye on it now and then.
