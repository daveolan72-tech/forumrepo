Community Guidelines

This forum works because people treat each other well. Here is what we expect from everyone who posts here.

Be respectful

Disagree with the idea, not the person. You can push back hard on an argument without insulting whoever made it.

  • No personal attacks, name calling or harassment
  • No hate speech or slurs of any kind
  • No threats, doxxing or sharing someone's private information
  • Keep it civil even when a thread gets heated

Post in the right place

A little effort up front makes topics easier for everyone to find and answer.

  • Search first, someone may have already asked the same thing
  • Pick the category that fits, General, Help and Support, Showcase, Feedback or Off-topic
  • Write a clear title that says what the topic is about
  • Give enough detail that people can actually help you

Keep it clean

  • No spam, advertising or referral links
  • No adult or graphic content
  • No pirated software, cracks or stolen material
  • Do not post the same topic in several categories

Credit your sources

If you are quoting an article, copying code or sharing someone else's work, say where it came from and link back to it. Do not pass off other people's work as your own.

One account per person

Use a single account. Do not create extra accounts to upvote your own posts, get around a ban, or pretend to be someone else.

What happens if you break the rules

Moderators can edit or remove posts that break these guidelines. Depending on how serious it is, you may get a warning, a temporary suspension, or a permanent ban.

If you think a decision was wrong, reach out through the Contact page and we will take another look.

Report something

Seen a post that breaks these rules? Use the report option on the post, or send us a message through the Contact page. Reports stay private.
